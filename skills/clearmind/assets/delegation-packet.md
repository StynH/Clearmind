## Worker instructions

Confirm that the necessary context, skills, tools, and source state are accessible.
At startup, consider whether the assignment contains a useful further split; propose
it to the parent unless nested delegation is explicitly authorized. Keep the
assignment bounded and coordinate ownership changes before editing other surfaces.

Follow ClearMind's quiet-product rules, engineering judgment, observed verification,
and critical review. Work in short cycles. Report missing context, contract changes,
permission boundaries, or unsupported assumptions instead of guessing. Review-only
assignments remain read-only unless the parent explicitly authorizes an edit.
